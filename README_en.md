# Shenzhen Elderly Park Guide

> A WeChat mini program designed for elderly residents in Shenzhen, currently under development.

## Core Purpose

The "Shenzhen Elderly Park Guide" is a WeChat mini program designed for elderly residents in Shenzhen. It aims to provide convenient and thoughtful park guidance services, helping the elderly quickly find green spaces suitable for leisure, exercise, and socializing.

## Background

As Shenzhen's population ages, the elderly's demand for outdoor activity venues continues to grow. However, park information is often scattered across multiple channels and rarely organized around the actual needs of the elderly (such as accessible pathways, resting benches, and shaded facilities). When choosing activity venues, the elderly often face inconvenience due to the lack of age-friendly information.

## Development Goals

- Gather basic information about major parks in Shenzhen and provide a unified, easy-to-read park guidance entry point;
- Provide feature filtering dimensions tailored to the needs of the elderly, such as accessible pathways, ample resting benches, shaded pavilions, senior fitness equipment, and quiet walking paths;
- Lower the usage threshold through age-friendly design (larger fonts, high contrast, and simple interactions);
- Help the elderly quickly locate parks that suit them through search and multi-tag filtering.

## Park Feature Module Planning

Standardized, age-friendly information modules are planned for each park to help the elderly obtain the information they need quickly and clearly. The modules are planned as follows:

### 1. Park Preview Images
Provide multi-angle, real-world preview images for each park, allowing the elderly to intuitively understand the park's environment and facilities without visiting in person, helping them determine whether the park meets their needs.

### 2. Comprehensive Rating
Provide a comprehensive rating on a 5.0-point scale for each park, intuitively reflecting its overall quality and popularity, and helping the elderly compare different parks at a glance.

### 3. Precise Geolocation
Provide the precise coordinates and map location of each park, helping the elderly accurately determine its position while clearly displaying surrounding roads and main entrances on the map.

### 4. Core Feature Summary
Summarize each park's core features with concise tags (such as "ample seats | accessible-friendly | suitable for walking"), allowing the elderly to grasp key information at a glance.

### 5. Age-Friendly Rating Radar Chart
Rate each park's age-friendliness across multiple dimensions such as accessibility, resting facilities, shade, fitness, and quietness, and present the results visually in a radar chart to help the elderly understand its age-friendliness comprehensively.

### 6. Scientific Exercise Duration Suggestions
Provide scientific exercise duration and intensity suggestions based on the physical condition of the elderly, guiding them to maintain a reasonable balance between exercise and rest while advocating safe and moderate exercise.

### 7. Public Transportation Guidance
Provide public transportation guidance such as metro and bus routes, and integrate navigation software APIs to support one-tap navigation, helping the elderly conveniently plan their travel routes.

### 8. Peak Crowd Time Alerts
Mark peak and off-peak hours for each park, helping the elderly avoid crowds and choose relatively quiet and safe time periods to visit, thereby improving their overall experience.
